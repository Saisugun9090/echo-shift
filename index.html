A NOTE FROM YOUR FUTURE SELF

You can be in
two places at once.

  1. Move onto a switch.Use arrow keys, WASD or the arrow buttons. You can also tap a neighbouring tile.
  2. Cast an echo.Press Space. Your route is saved and you return to the start. Crystals reset for this new run.
  3. Work with your past.Every move advances your echoes one step. They stay at the end of their route, keeping switches pressed. Wait with the period key when you need to sync up.
  4. Find the exit.Collect every crystal in this run. A switch opens gates with the same letter. Two echoes fit in a chamber; a third replaces the oldest.

Echoes move first on each step. A gate must be held when you enter. Echoes replay freely through gates. Blocked moves do not advance time. R resets the chamber. Your best scores are saved on this device when storage is available.
