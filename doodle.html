    THE SHORT VERSION

    1. The artist chooses one of three words.
    2. Draw it live. No letters or spelling clues.
    3. Everyone else types guesses. Each correct player earns one point.
    4. The word stays secret until the timer ends. Then swap the artist.

    Each person draws once. Most points wins; ties share the win. The artist does not guess or score on their own turn.
